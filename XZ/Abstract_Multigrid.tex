\section{variational Formulation}

we consider the problem
\[
    Ax = b
\]
with $ A: V \rightarrow V'$, $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and $b \in (\mathbb{R}^n)'$

x and b do not live in the same vector spaces:

$x$ is in $V \cong \mathbb{R}^n$, and represent a primal state (temperature, pressure), and the natural norm is the energy norm $\|x\|^2 = x^{\top}Ax$

$b$ is in $V' \cong (\mathbb{R}^n)'$, and represent a source (force, flux), and the natural norm is the dual norm $\|b\|^2 = b^{\top}A^{-1}b$

% in particular x^Tb does not really make sense
