\section{variational Formulation}

we consider the problem
\[
    Ax = b
\]
with $ A: V \rightarrow V'$, $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and $b \in (\mathbb{R}^n)'$

x and b do not live in the same vector spaces:

$x$ is in $V \cong \mathbb{R}^n$, and represent a primal state (temperature, pressure), and the natural norm is the energy norm $\|x\|^2 = x^{\top}Ax$

$b$ is in $V' \cong (\mathbb{R}^n)'$, and represent a source (force, flux), and the natural norm is the dual norm $\|b\|^2 = b^{\top}A^{-1}b$

% in particular x^Tb does not really make sense

It is this problem that arise naturally from the weak formulation.

and the matrix assembled is ...

we solve
\[
    \text{Find } u \in V \text{ such that } \langle A u, v \rangle = \langle f, v \rangle \quad \forall v \in V
\]
% noting that Au is in V', so \langle A u, v \rangle is the real number (Au)(v)

The idea is to approximate the solution in the range of $P$, which means finding a $u_c \in V_c$, such that $u \approx Pu_c$

We are doing a Galerkin projection on $range(P) \in V$: 
$$\text{Find } u_P \in range(P) \text{ such that } \langle A P u_P, v_P \rangle = \langle f, v_P \rangle \quad \forall v_P \in range(P)$$

If we define $e_{approx} = u-u_P$, we can note that $\langle Ae_{approx}, v_P \rangle = 0$ for all $v_P$, ie the approximation error is orthogonal to $range(P)$

we then have
\begin{align*}
    \langle Ae, v_P \rangle = 0 \\
    \langle Au - Au_P, v_P \rangle = 0 \\
    \langle Au_P, v_P \rangle = \langle f, v_P \rangle \\
    \langle APu_c, Pv_c \rangle = \langle f, Pv_c \rangle \\
    \langle P'APu_c, v_c \rangle = \langle P'f, v_c \rangle \\
\end{align*}
